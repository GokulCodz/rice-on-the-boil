    Scope, honesty and sources

    Every number on this page is calculated by the model in physics.js unless a label says illustrative (chosen to show an idea, e.g. bubbles, flame flicker, grain arrangement) or observed (your own kitchen measurements). Grain fields are one-dimensional (equivalent sphere, cylinder or slab with the grain's volume-to-surface ratio); drawing them on a rice-grain outline (with shading and shine) is a display mapping. Parameters marked “illustrative” lie inside published ranges but were not measured for one rice variety. The model is not fitted to cooking-time data.

    1. Welty, Wicks, Wilson & Rorrer, Fundamentals of Momentum, Heat, and Mass Transfer, 5th ed., Wiley — control-volume balances (Ch. 4, 6), conduction and diffusion. Course slides: Conservation and Transport Equations (S. Bhattacharyya), slides 10, 18, 39–50, 56, 83.
    2. Briffaz, A. et al. (2014) Modelling of water transport and swelling associated with starch gelatinization during rice cooking. J. Food Eng. 121, 143–151. — apparent D in native and gelatinized starch.
    3. Briffaz, A. et al. (2014) Modelling of brown rice and limited-water cooking modes and its potential use for texture prediction. J. Food Eng. 141, 99–106. — brown rice D.
    4. Bello, M., Tolaba, M. P., Suárez, C. — water absorption of rice during soaking; swelling-sphere diffusion model with Ea ≈ 32.5 kJ/mol (LWT 2004; J. Food Eng. 2010).
    5. Lund, D. (1984) Influence of time, temperature, moisture, ingredients and processing conditions on starch gelatinization. CRC Crit. Rev. Food Sci. Nutr. 20, 249–273. Donovan, J. W. (1979) Phase transitions of the starch–water system. Biopolymers 18, 263–275.
    6. Choi, Y. & Okos, M. R. (1986) Effects of temperature and composition on the thermal properties of foods. In Food Engineering and Process Applications, Elsevier. — component thermal properties.
    7. Crank, J. (1975) The Mathematics of Diffusion, 2nd ed., OUP; Carslaw, H. S. & Jaeger, J. C. (1959) Conduction of Heat in Solids, OUP — analytical checks. Churchill, S. W. (1983) natural convection from spheres. Antoine constants for water (NIST WebBook).
    8. ISO 14864:1998 Rice — Evaluation of gelatinization time of kernels during cooking (glass-plate method), used for the optional observation panel.
